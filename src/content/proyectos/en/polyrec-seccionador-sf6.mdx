## Challenge

The substation did not have its own electrical protection for the transformer; the feeder came from the free zone park substation, with no way to isolate its own circuit or perform maintenance without affecting the park.

## Approach

- Installation of the plant's own SF6 disconnector (SOCOL).
- Protection of the transformer and the main feeder.
- Ability to switch off power at will.

## Results

Own electrical protection and switching autonomy for maintenance tasks.
