## Challenge

The loss of process variable information prevented the online indicators of the drying system from being updated.

## Approach

- Implementation of real-time monitoring with new control technologies.
- Installation in compliance with RETIE regulations.

## Results

Real-time traceability of drying process variables and indicators.
