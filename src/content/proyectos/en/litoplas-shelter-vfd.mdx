## Challenge

The variable frequency drives of the extruders were burning out due to overtemperature, causing recurring machine stoppages and affecting production continuity.

## Approach

- Design and implementation of a climate-controlled shelter dedicated to the variable frequency drives.
- Coverage of extruders 21, 9, and 5.
- Preservation of the optimal operating environment for the VFDs within the thermal range required by the manufacturer.

## Results

Controlled environment and effective protection of the drives, with elimination of overtemperature faults and increased line availability.
