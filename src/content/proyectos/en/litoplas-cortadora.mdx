## Challenge

Electronic failures in the cutter's controller caused information loss and recurring machine stops.

## Approach

- Upgrade of the machine's controller.
- Upgrade of the operator HMI.
- Development of new control logic adapted to the characteristics of the cutter.

## Results

Upgraded control and increased machine availability, with a reduction in stops related to controller failures.
