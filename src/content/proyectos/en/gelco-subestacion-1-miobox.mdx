## Challenge

The substation had equipment and infrastructure outside the RETIE standard, with spontaneous energy losses.

## Approach

- Installation of 440/220 Vac distribution panels with capacitor bank.
- Energy measurement linked to the MIOBOX platform.
- Installation in compliance with RETIE regulations.

## Results

Renovated substation, with energy management by circuit.
