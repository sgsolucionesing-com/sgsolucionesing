## Challenge

The substation had equipment and infrastructure not compliant with RETIE standard, deficient installations, and a corrosive environment, with unexpected energy losses.

## Approach

- Installation of 440 Vac and 220 Vac distribution panels, each with a capacitor bank for reactive power control.
- Per-circuit energy measurement linked to the MIOBOX platform.
- Implementation of a cable tray and PVC conduit system.
- Installation compliant with RETIE standard.

## Results

Substation completely renovated and fully metered, with real-time per-circuit energy management.
