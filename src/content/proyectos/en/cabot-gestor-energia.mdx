## Challenge

To modernize the motor control center, migrating from E1 to Rockwell E300 technology, and implement energy and asset management tools at the Cartagena plant.

## Approach

- Update of smart loads and power monitors.
- Incorporation of Powerflex 525 and 753 variable speed drives.
- Installation of SMC-50 soft starter.
- Deployment of industrial Ethernet network with Stratix 5800 switches.
- Implementation of ControlLogix 5580 process unit.
- Expansion of the MIOBOX energy management platform and plant historian.
- Specialized engineering and training for plant personnel.

## Results

Expanded energy management platform, with real-time monitoring of plant loads.
