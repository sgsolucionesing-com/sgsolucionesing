## Challenge

The main substation did not have its own line protection system against temporary faults; its only protection was fuses.

## Approach

- Installation of its own recloser system, according to the integration designs of the operator Air-e.
- Fault detection and isolation with automatic circuit reconnection.

## Results

The installations are currently in progress. Once operational, it is expected to provide greater supply reliability, shorter outage durations, reduced interruption costs, and management with remote monitoring.
