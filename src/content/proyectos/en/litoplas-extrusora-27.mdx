## Challenge

Extruder 27's control system was obsolete, with low production affecting process availability and reliability.

## Approach

- Implementation of a new-generation Siemens S7-1500 PLC.
- Incorporation of distributed I/O ET200SP.
- Installation of a Siemens HMI for machine operation.
- Integration of a motor driver for machine motor control.

## Results

Modernized control, updated operation via the HMI, and increased reliability of the production process.
