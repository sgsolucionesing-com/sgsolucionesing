## Challenge

The consumption load increase from 2.5 to 3.5 MVA had not been regularized before the energy operator.

## Approach

- Regularization of the load increase from 2.5 to 3.5 MVA before the operator.
- Installation of the medium voltage metering system under current regulations.
- Compliance with the RETIE standard.

## Results

Regularized load and certified metering system before the energy operator.
