## Challenge

The plant had recurring failures in Allen Bradley E1 Plus modules, a technology being discontinued, coupled with an outdated plant SCADA that limited the utilization of available technology.

## Approach

- Migration from E1 Plus relays to Allen Bradley E300 intelligent relays.
- Update of the plant SCADA system.

## Results

Greater system reliability, simplified maintenance, future integration with new technologies, and real-time data monitoring, control, and analysis.
