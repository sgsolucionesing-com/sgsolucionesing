## Challenge

The InTouch SCADA was running on a damaged and non-functional Magelis panel PC, compromising process supervision.

## Approach

- Migration of the SCADA system to a new Siemens supervisory platform.
- Implementation on a new Siemens panel PC.
- Development in TIA Portal for operational continuity of the system.

## Results

Operational and reliable supervisory system, with full recovery of process visibility.
